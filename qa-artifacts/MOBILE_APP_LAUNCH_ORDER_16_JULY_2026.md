# THE C1RCLE — Superseded Mobile Launch Order

Date superseded: 17 July 2026

This file is retained only so old links do not break. It is **not** a launch checklist and must not be used for a GO decision.

The earlier version made unsupported `LAUNCH-READY` claims for legal/privacy/store compliance and database/infrastructure readiness, and incorrectly stated that completing nine engineering tasks would clear the app for immediate store submission. Live source/config inspection, current official platform requirements, and the available QA evidence do not support those conclusions.

Use the single canonical launch-control file:

[MOBILE_PRODUCTION_READINESS_AUDIT_2026-07-16.md](./MOBILE_PRODUCTION_READINESS_AUDIT_2026-07-16.md)

The nine engineering tasks from the former plan remain valid open work and are preserved in the canonical audit. They are necessary but not sufficient. The canonical audit additionally controls Android and iOS release binaries, Google Play and App Store Connect, privacy/deletion, UGC/age safety, India legal/tax/regulatory readiness, security, operations, cost controls, evidence, and approvals.

Current release decision: **NO-GO**.
